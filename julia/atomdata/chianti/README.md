CHIANTI database files for H I, He I and He II (Dere et al. 1997, A&AS 125, 149; Del Zanna et al. 2021, ApJ 909, 38),
copied from the git mirror https://github.com/wtbarnes/chianti-database (branch main, last commit 2024-09-13; tags to v10.1),
paths h/h_1, he/he_1, he/he_2. The footers of each file give the original data sources: H I collisions Anderson et al. 2000,
J. Phys. B 33, 1255 (revised 2002); He I collisions Sawey & Berrington 1993 and Bray et al. 2000; He II collisions Ballance
(R-matrix with pseudostates, private communication to CHIANTI). Read by julia/ModelAtom.jl. Note: the H I and He II .scups
files contain transitions from 1s and 2s only (none from 2p).
