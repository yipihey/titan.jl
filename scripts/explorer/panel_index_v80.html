Pop III Run Explorer

Profiles, track and computing cost of the from-scratch runs. Since 11 Nov, 21:00 three runs are active, each on 4 pinned threads, with the CMB and the diluted light of the star incident on the H₂ lines and with HD chemistry and cooling: b224, c128 and f128. b224 is the new baseline: it takes base64 at 0.045 M☉ (step 26 000) onto a fixed mesh of 224 zones, 3 % wide in radius from 3 to 1000 R☉, where the surface and the accretion shock are, and coarse in the core and the envelope. Without HD this mesh reproduces full6 between 2.3 and 7.2 M☉ within 2 to 5 % in radius, with no failed step; with HD a test on this mesh flashed below 0.63 M☉, with excursions of 20 to 50 % in radius, and was quiet after that, within 3 to 7 % of two test meshes that resolve the shock from 1.3 M☉ on. At 2.40 M☉ the time step of that test fell to the time the gas needs to cross one zone at the shock, the artefact of the step rule known from the resolved-shock meshes; a copy of the test with the rule applied after the chemistry step runs on (5.1 M☉, 67 R☉ at 00:53, 12 Nov). b224 itself swelled to 111 R☉ at 0.60 M☉, collapsed at 0.61 to 0.63 M☉ onto the branch of the test (56 to 58 R☉ from 0.9 to 1.3 M☉), and was held at 1.33 to 1.35 M☉ by the same artefact from 22:15 to 23:53 on 11 Nov (those frames show a stalled run, not a star that stopped accreting). It left the stall with a second swelling (96 R☉ at 1.36 M☉), came back to 60 R☉ at 1.5 M☉ and has run without further stalls since: 3.05 M☉, 65 R☉ at 00:53, 12 Nov. The mesh of b224 also samples the infalling envelope poorly (1 to 4 zones per half decade of radius beyond 3000 R☉); a mesh of 200 zones that follows both radius and mass and moves with the star is in its first test, from the same checkpoint as b224 (0.92 M☉ at 00:53, 12 Nov). It conserves the total mass to 1 part in 10¹², keeps eight zones of 2 % across the accretion shock and follows b224 through the flashing phase, but it swells again at 0.91 M☉ where b224 did not, and a fixed mesh with a fine envelope shows that b224's coarse envelope lets about 9 % too little mass through 10⁴ R☉ and 21 % too little through 3×10⁴ R☉, while the moving mesh lets 20 % too much through 3×10⁴ R☉ (its envelope moves faster than the gas); a version with the envelope frozen beyond 3000 R☉ is under test. The code is in the repository, switched off by default; it is not in this explorer yet. base64, the first baseline from the initial conditions on 64 zones (2×10⁴ M☉ cloud, continuum transport from log n_c = 14), was stopped on 11 Nov, 20:01 at 1.78 M☉: on 64 fixed zones the radius of the star stays on one mesh interface (144 R☉) and the accretion shock is one zone. nohd64, the same run without HD (with HD the core is at 220 K when log n_c = 7.8, without it at 676 K, and the collapse comes 7×10⁶ yr earlier); c128, the production line continued from 141.5 M☉ on 128 zones (146 zones merged two to one between 11 and 1500 R☉); and f128, full6 continued from 14.3 M☉ on 128 zones (its 380 zones merged three to one). Caution for nohd64 after 0.5 M☉ (11 Nov, 14:39): on the fixed 64-zone mesh one zone, 39 % wide in radius, holds the whole photosphere. The star there is several times larger than in full6 at the same mass, its luminosity comes in flashes, and most steps fail to converge; the radius and luminosity of that phase are not results. full3 and full4 were stopped at 10.5 and 10.9 M☉; full6 and c146 end where their continuations begin. The earlier runs: full4 switches continuum radiation transport on at log n_c = 14, before the continuum matters; full3 uses the fitted continuum cooling with an escape factor and switches radiation on at log n_c = 18.5. The two are the same run up to log n_c = 14 (step 9500). After that: core growth, then accretion on the fixed mesh. full6 is a new start from the initial conditions with the cloud extended from 10⁴ to 2×10⁴ M☉ (outer edge 167 pc instead of 55 pc), continuum transport from log n_c = 14, and artificial viscosity only in radial compression (shocks), not in stretched free fall. full2 ran without continuum cooling or radiation in the dense core and is valid only below log n_c ≈ 14; it was stopped at log n_c = 22.4. The reference history is the earlier production run (0.5 to 97 M☉). fx3 is the same production run from 102.7 to 105.9 M☉ (10 Nov) on its mesh of 286 zones, recorded in full since it was restarted with shock-only viscosity, zone-local chemistry sub-steps and the faster line solver; its photosphere pulsates and the cycle is not resolved. c146 continues that run from 105.4 M☉ on a mesh of 146 zones (zones merged three to one outside 30 R☉); it is recorded here from 106 M☉ and was the production line from 10 Nov, 22:55 to 11 Nov, 11:00 (141.5 M☉). On this mesh the cycle is gone: the photosphere stays on one mesh interface (5640 R☉), while the envelope between the star and the photosphere gains mass at about 2×10⁻³ M☉ per year, the same rate as in fx3. Overlay matches frames by protostar mass once there is a protostar, and by central density before.

Optical depth in these panels is the grey continuum optical depth the run itself uses: τR(r) = ∫ χR ρ dr from r out to the outer mesh boundary, where τR = 0 (gas beyond the mesh does not count). χR is the Rosseland mean of the extinction, absorption plus Rayleigh and Thomson scattering, from the equilibrium opacity table and corrected in the non-equilibrium zones for the zone's own composition. The dotted line is τR = 2/3, the run's photosphere (r_ph, T_eff). It is not a line optical depth: the H₂ lines have their own transfer, line by line. Wavelength-dependent depths are in the spectrum section below.

Computed after the fact from the snapshot nearest to the frame; nothing feeds back into the run. Continuum: static spherical transfer at 216 wavelengths (0.05 to 1000 μm) on the snapshot's density, gas temperature and non-equilibrium composition. Opacity: collision-induced absorption of H₂, H⁻ bound–free and free–free, H bound–free and free–free, Rayleigh and Thomson scattering (coherent). No atomic lines and no helium opacity. Bound–free continua emit by recombination of the zone's own electrons; everything else emits thermally at the gas temperature. Edge is the outer mesh boundary: what leaves the computed volume. Photosphere is the run's grey τR = 2/3 surface. H₂ lines are the run's own line transfer, drawn as sticks whose height is each line's luminosity; they see no continuum extinction. The right panel shows where the monochromatic radial optical depth τλ = ∫ (κλ + σλ) ρ dr, from the mesh edge inwards, reaches 2/3; the dotted line is the grey photosphere. Check: the temperature structure is the grey run's, so ∫Lνdν need not equal the run's luminosity; the chips give both. They disagree strongly where the photosphere sits inside the accretion shock and the optical depth jumps from below 2/3 to hundreds within one zone: there the spectrum at the photosphere is not resolved.

Nuclear burning

Luminosity by burning process (L☉), selected run

Recomputed from each snapshot's temperature, density and nuclear abundances with the run's own reaction network (zones above 3×10⁵ K); the five parts add up to the nuclear luminosity the run recorded, net of neutrino losses. The x axis is the one chosen for the track. Hydrogen (pp chain to ³He) is p+p and pep together with the d(p,γ)³He that follows them. Deuterium is the burning of deuterium that p+p did not make in the same zone: the deuterium the gas came with. ³He is ³He+³He, ³He+⁴He and the ⁷Be, ⁷Li and ⁸B branches, whatever the origin of the ³He. Onset marks (D, pp, CNO): the first snapshot from which the process supplies more than 0.1 % of the luminosity at the photosphere in three snapshots in a row. The luminosity used is the running median over 100 snapshots to either side, because the photosphere can pulsate; the search goes through every recorded snapshot, not only the frames shown here. An onset that lies before the first recorded snapshot cannot be dated from this record. The chart reaches six decades below the onset level; a process weaker than that is off the bottom (its value is in the readout) until “All decades” is ticked. Runs recorded before the abundances were stored (reference, full2) have no abundance panels and no burning chart.
