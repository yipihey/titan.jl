Profiles, track and computing cost of the from-scratch Pop III protostar runs. Goal: follow the accretion until the cloud is accreted (300 M☉) or the star stops the inflow. Production is t_c128c: the 128-zone line c128 (from 141.5 M☉), continued since 269.9 M☉ with the chemistry coupled into the hydrodynamic Newton step in the stiff band (14 Nov; time steps about 6× longer than the split-chemistry c128, which it replaces, at equal results within a few per cent). Between 263 and 270 M☉ the hot envelope inside the photosphere drained and collapsed onto the star (infall up to 1200 km/s). t_ec and t_b224c8 are the 224-zone references of the 10 M☉ phase. The page checks for new data every two minutes.

History of the runs to 12 Nov

Profiles, track and computing cost of the from-scratch runs. Since 11 Nov, 21:00 three runs are active, each on 4 pinned threads, with the CMB and the diluted light of the star incident on the H₂ lines and with HD chemistry and cooling: b224, c128 and f128. b224 is the new baseline: it takes base64 at 0.045 M☉ (step 26 000) onto a fixed mesh of 224 zones, 3 % wide in radius from 3 to 1000 R☉, where the surface and the accretion shock are, and coarse in the core and the envelope. Without HD this mesh reproduces full6 between 2.3 and 7.2 M☉ within 2 to 5 % in radius, with no failed step; with HD a test on this mesh flashed below 0.63 M☉, with excursions of 20 to 50 % in radius, and was quiet after that, within 3 to 7 % of two test meshes that resolve the shock from 1.3 M☉ on. At 2.40 M☉ the time step of that test fell to the time the gas needs to cross one zone at the shock, the artefact of the step rule known from the resolved-shock meshes; a copy of the test with the rule applied after the chemistry step runs on (5.1 M☉, 67 R☉ at 00:53, 12 Nov). b224 itself swelled to 111 R☉ at 0.60 M☉, collapsed at 0.61 to 0.63 M☉ onto the branch of the test (56 to 58 R☉ from 0.9 to 1.3 M☉), and was held at 1.33 to 1.35 M☉ by the same artefact from 22:15 to 23:53 on 11 Nov (those frames show a stalled run, not a star that stopped accreting). It left the stall with a second swelling (96 R☉ at 1.36 M☉), came back to 60 R☉ at 1.5 M☉ and ran without further stalls to 4.20 M☉; since 02:15 on 12 Nov it is held there by the same step-rule artefact (4.22 M☉ at 06:27; frames after that show a stalled run, not a star that stopped accreting). The copy with the rule applied after the chemistry step passed this mass without a stall. The mesh of b224 also samples the infalling envelope poorly (1 to 4 zones per half decade of radius beyond 3000 R☉); a mesh of 200 zones that follows both radius and mass and moves with the star is in its first test, from the same checkpoint as b224 (0.92 M☉ at 00:53, 12 Nov). It conserves the total mass to 1 part in 10¹², keeps eight zones of 2 % across the accretion shock and follows b224 through the flashing phase, but it swells again at 0.91 M☉ where b224 did not, and a fixed mesh with a fine envelope shows that b224's coarse envelope lets about 9 % too little mass through 10⁴ R☉ and 21 % too little through 3×10⁴ R☉, while the moving mesh lets 20 % too much through 3×10⁴ R☉ (its envelope moves faster than the gas); a version with the envelope frozen beyond 3000 R☉ failed to converge at 0.107 M☉ with the post-chemistry step rule on and passed that point with the rule off. The code is in the repository, switched off by default; it is not in this explorer yet. Emission (12 Nov): non-LTE H I, He I and He II atoms (tested against Storey & Hummer 1995 and Porter et al. 2012) applied to c128 snapshots from 142 to 190 M☉ find the envelope between the star and the shell in LTE and fully ionized; the recombination lines made there, He II 1640 included, are buried under a continuum optical depth of about 10⁴, and the lines that escape come from the cool infall outside the 3400 K photosphere (Hα about 280 L☉, 4×10⁻⁵ of the luminosity). base64, the first baseline from the initial conditions on 64 zones (2×10⁴ M☉ cloud, continuum transport from log n_c = 14), was stopped on 11 Nov, 20:01 at 1.78 M☉: on 64 fixed zones the radius of the star stays on one mesh interface (144 R☉) and the accretion shock is one zone. nohd64, the same run without HD (with HD the core is at 220 K when log n_c = 7.8, without it at 676 K, and the collapse comes 7×10⁶ yr earlier); c128, the production line continued from 141.5 M☉ on 128 zones (146 zones merged two to one between 11 and 1500 R☉); and f128, full6 continued from 14.3 M☉ on 128 zones (its 380 zones merged three to one). Caution for nohd64 after 0.5 M☉ (11 Nov, 14:39): on the fixed 64-zone mesh one zone, 39 % wide in radius, holds the whole photosphere. The star there is several times larger than in full6 at the same mass, its luminosity comes in flashes, and most steps fail to converge; the radius and luminosity of that phase are not results. full3 and full4 were stopped at 10.5 and 10.9 M☉; full6 and c146 end where their continuations begin. The earlier runs: full4 switches continuum radiation transport on at log n_c = 14, before the continuum matters; full3 uses the fitted continuum cooling with an escape factor and switches radiation on at log n_c = 18.5. The two are the same run up to log n_c = 14 (step 9500). After that: core growth, then accretion on the fixed mesh. full6 is a new start from the initial conditions with the cloud extended from 10⁴ to 2×10⁴ M☉ (outer edge 167 pc instead of 55 pc), continuum transport from log n_c = 14, and artificial viscosity only in radial compression (shocks), not in stretched free fall. full2 ran without continuum cooling or radiation in the dense core and is valid only below log n_c ≈ 14; it was stopped at log n_c = 22.4. The reference history is the earlier production run (0.5 to 97 M☉). fx3 is the same production run from 102.7 to 105.9 M☉ (10 Nov) on its mesh of 286 zones, recorded in full since it was restarted with shock-only viscosity, zone-local chemistry sub-steps and the faster line solver; its photosphere pulsates and the cycle is not resolved. c146 continues that run from 105.4 M☉ on a mesh of 146 zones (zones merged three to one outside 30 R☉); it is recorded here from 106 M☉ and was the production line from 10 Nov, 22:55 to 11 Nov, 11:00 (141.5 M☉). On this mesh the cycle is gone: the photosphere stays on one mesh interface (5640 R☉), while the envelope between the star and the photosphere gains mass at about 2×10⁻³ M☉ per year, the same rate as in fx3. Overlay matches frames by protostar mass once there is a protostar, and by central density before.

Optical depth in these panels is the grey continuum optical depth the run itself uses: τR(r) = ∫ χR ρ dr from r out to the outer mesh boundary, where τR = 0 (gas beyond the mesh does not count). χR is the Rosseland mean of the extinction, absorption plus Rayleigh and Thomson scattering, from the equilibrium opacity table and corrected in the non-equilibrium zones for the zone's own composition. The dotted line is τR = 2/3, the run's photosphere (r_ph, T_eff). It is not a line optical depth: the H₂ lines have their own transfer, line by line. Wavelength-dependent depths are in the spectrum section below.

Observable spectrum

ν Lν (L☉) against wavelength (μm)

Leaving the grid (solid): Lν leaving the outermost zone of the mesh, the spectrum an observer would receive (no gas beyond the mesh). Through the photosphere (dashed): Lν = 16π² r² Hν, the net flux through the interface where the run's grey Rosseland optical depth from the edge reaches 2/3, i.e. what emerges from just above the photosphere before the envelope outside reprocesses it. Both come from the same static spherical formal solution of the snapshot nearest to the frame (step shown in the chips). With the with lines source the continuum includes H I, He I, He II bound–free and free–free opacity, and each line is drawn as a spike from the continuum to the extreme of its emergent profile at the edge (up: emission, down: absorption; the profile itself, with the infall, is in the diagnostics below); for spectra computed since 14 Nov the lines are also transferred to the photosphere sphere (dashed spikes: Lν = 16π² r² Hν through rph in the same observer-frame solution), so the difference between the solid and the dashed spikes is what the envelope outside the photosphere adds or absorbs; older spectra have the photosphere continuum only. The continuum only source has more snapshots (H₂ lines there are sticks of line luminosity, in the diagnostics). Dotted: blackbody of the run's Teff at its photosphere radius. If the chips flag a luminosity mismatch, the spectrum radiates an unresolved zone and is not a prediction for that snapshot.

Continuum spectrum

ν Lν (L☉) against wavelength (μm)

Radius where τλ = 2/3 (R☉) against wavelength (μm)

Optical depth by process against wavelength (μm)

Computed after the fact from the snapshot nearest to the frame; nothing feeds back into the run. Continuum: static spherical transfer at 216 wavelengths (0.05 to 1000 μm) on the snapshot's density, gas temperature and non-equilibrium composition. Opacity: collision-induced absorption of H₂ (H₂–H₂, H₂–He, H₂–H), H⁻ bound–free and free–free, H bound–free (levels n ≤ 30) and free–free, Rayleigh scattering on H₂ and H and Thomson scattering (coherent). No lines in the continuum transfer, neither in absorption nor in emission: no H I lines (Lyman, Balmer, Paschen, …), no He I (584 Å, 10830, 5876 Å) or He II (304 Å, 1640, 4686 Å) lines, no H₂ or HD lines. Helium enters only through H₂–He collision-induced absorption: no He bound–free or free–free continua (the wavelength grid ends at 0.05 μm, so the He II and most of the He I continua lie outside it anyway). Bound–free continua emit by recombination of the zone's own electrons; everything else emits thermally at the gas temperature. Edge is the outer mesh boundary: what leaves the computed volume. Photosphere is the run's grey τR = 2/3 surface. H₂ lines (rovibrational quadrupole lines of the run's level model) come from the run's own line transfer, in emission only, drawn as sticks whose height is each line's luminosity (L☉, not ν Lν), scaled per snapshot to the gas's collisional line cooling; they see no continuum extinction and do not absorb the continuum. HD lines are not computed. The right panel shows where the monochromatic radial optical depth τλ = ∫ (κλ + σλ) ρ dr, from the mesh edge inwards, reaches 2/3; the dotted line is the grey photosphere. Check: the temperature structure is the grey run's, so ∫Lνdν need not equal the run's luminosity; the chips give both. They disagree strongly where the photosphere sits inside the accretion shock and the optical depth jumps from below 2/3 to hundreds within one zone: there the spectrum at the photosphere is not resolved. Optical depth by process (third panel): the optical depth of each opacity process accumulated from the outer mesh boundary inwards, with the same opacities and points as the continuum transfer, either down to the radius where the monochromatic τλ reaches 2/3 (the processes then sum to 2/3: they are what makes the photosphere at that wavelength) or down to the run's grey photosphere rph. It is extinction (absorption with stimulated emission subtracted, plus scattering) and contains no lines; the thin line is the sum of the processes.

Radial line-centre optical depth τ0 against radius (R☉)

Computed after the fact (julia/SpecFull.jl, snapfull.jl) from the snapshot nearest to the frame, for fewer snapshots than the continuum above (about one minute each). Continuum 1000 to 0.01 μm: H₂ collision-induced absorption, H⁻ bound–free and free–free, bound–free of every level of H I (n ≤ 20), He I (CHIANTI terms + hydrogenic shells) and He II (n ≤ 20) with their own recombination emission, free–free on H⁺, He⁺ and He²⁺, Rayleigh and Thomson scattering. Level populations: non-LTE statistical equilibrium of the three atoms at the snapshot's T and n_e, with the run's ionisation (ground state and ion fixed to the snapshot), in the continuum J of this transfer and Sobolev escape in the snapshot's velocity field; H₂ from the run's own line transfer (ALI). Lines (37: Lyman, Balmer, Paschen, Brackett; He I 584 … 20581; He II 304 … 10124; H₂ 1–0, 2–1 and 0–0) as opacity and emissivity with Voigt profiles (thermal + 2 km/s microturbulence, radiative damping, electron impact, the ions' linear Stark pattern for H I and He II), solved in the observer frame along the tangent rays with the infall: absorption appears where the line's source function is below the light behind it, emission otherwise; infall gives redshifted absorption. EW < 0 is emission. Photosphere: where the run's mesh puts the whole photosphere into one zone (c128), that zone is split into sub-layers on the run's grey τ scale with a grey Eddington T(τ), normalised so that the continuum carries the run's luminosity (the factor is shown); this is a model assumption, not resolved physics. Not included: HD lines (no level data in the code yet), line photons scattered by electrons, the line J in the populations beyond Sobolev, lines feeding each other's windows; a window is ±600 km/s, so far Stark wings of Lyα are cut.

Photons ahead of the photosphere

Ionizing and Lyman–Werner photon rates (s⁻¹), selected run, against log M*

Post-processed from every snapshot (scripts: julia/ionfs_post.jl, branch ionfs-v2): the frequency-resolved formal solution of the emergent spectrum (sub-zone photosphere, source from grey τ > 3000, so photons leaking from below the photosphere count), followed by a photon-conserving ray through the infall with H, He and H₂ absorbers and H₂ self-shielding. “Emitted” leaves the photosphere; “at r_a” reaches r_a = max(r_ph + 10 R☉, 2 r_ph). The break-out alarm fires when Q_H at r_a exceeds 10⁴² s⁻¹ or the LW dissociation time at r_a falls below 10 flow times; the coupled ray (IONFSIMP) is then switched on in the run.

Nuclear burning

Luminosity by burning process (L☉), selected run

Recomputed from each snapshot's temperature, density and nuclear abundances with the run's own reaction network (zones above 3×10⁵ K); the five parts add up to the nuclear luminosity the run recorded, net of neutrino losses. The x axis is the one chosen for the track. Hydrogen (pp chain to ³He) is p+p and pep together with the d(p,γ)³He that follows them. Deuterium is the burning of deuterium that p+p did not make in the same zone: the deuterium the gas came with. ³He is ³He+³He, ³He+⁴He and the ⁷Be, ⁷Li and ⁸B branches, whatever the origin of the ³He. Onset marks (D, pp, CNO): the first snapshot from which the process supplies more than 0.1 % of the luminosity at the photosphere in three snapshots in a row. The luminosity used is the running median over 100 snapshots to either side, because the photosphere can pulsate; the search goes through every recorded snapshot, not only the frames shown here. An onset that lies before the first recorded snapshot cannot be dated from this record. The chart reaches six decades below the onset level; a process weaker than that is off the bottom (its value is in the readout) until “All decades” is ticked. Runs recorded before the abundances were stored (reference, full2) have no abundance panels and no burning chart.
